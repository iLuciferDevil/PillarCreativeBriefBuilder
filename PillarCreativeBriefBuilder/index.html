Performance Creative Brief Builder

A lightweight handoff from Growth to Katie’s Brand/Creative team. The brief should explain the business problem, the hypothesis, exactly what is needed, and how Growth will judge the test.

Growth → Brand / Creative

0. Brief setup

Enough context to make the brief identifiable later.

1. Business objective

State the outcome, not the creative deliverable.

2. Performance problem / opportunity

What is the data telling us that triggered this request?

3. Audience

If we do not have a validated customer insight, say NA rather than inventing one.

4. Hypothesis

Use: If we [change/test] for [audience], we expect [outcome] because [reason].

5. Message / angle

Keep this directional. Katie’s team should still have room to solve the creative problem.

6. Creative requirement

This is the actual ask to Katie.

7. What are we testing?

One primary variable per test whenever possible.

8. Measurement

The brief is incomplete without a baseline and decision rule.

9. References

Links are better than screenshots floating around Slack.

10. Post-test readout

Growth completes this after the test. Keeping it in the same brief builds a usable learning library.

Who owns what

GrowthSiddharth / Hafie owns problem, hypothesis, ask, KPI and test decision.
DataKeerthana can validate baseline, trend and supporting evidence.
Brand / CreativeKatie owns creative solution, execution quality and brand consistency.
